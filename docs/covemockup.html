What "Cove" actually is — and what your version will look like

Cove isn't a workout tracker. It's a private, ad-free place to save the pieces of your life — photos, notes, links, voice memos — sorted into themed spaces called Coves, instead of one endless feed. These five screens are the visual target: what your app should feel like once the MVP is done.

What makes Cove Cove

Coves, not a feed

Content is organized into themed collections you create, not sorted into one infinite chronological scroll. This is the one architectural decision everything else follows from.

Multi-type entries

A single entry can be a photo, a video, a link, a note, a voice memo, or a song — one flexible data model, not five separate ones.

Private-first

Everything defaults to visible only to you. Sharing is opt-in, per-Cove, and invite-based — never public by default.

Search over memories

Full-text (and eventually image-content) search across everything you've saved, scoped to a Cove or across all of them.

No ads, no algorithm

No ranking, no engagement loop, no monetization surface — the calm-tech stance that's core to the product, not a footnote.

Installs like a native app

Full-screen, home-screen icon, works offline for cached content — even before it's a "real" App Store app.
